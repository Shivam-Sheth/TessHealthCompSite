# PRODUCT OVERVIEW SECTION

## Section headline
> **One record. Every person in the circle.**

## One-paragraph product description
Tessera Health is a shared health record and AI coordination platform for home care. A patient creates one record — their medications, conditions, allergies, lab results, vitals, and care logs. They share it with their visiting nurse, their family doctor, and family members using a simple code. Everyone sees the same, real-time picture. And an embedded AI agent watches every observation, routes every alert, and briefs every team member — automatically.

## Extended product description (for About page / press)
Tessera Health is an AI-powered care coordination platform built for the way healthcare actually happens in most Indian families: a family doctor who makes house calls, a visiting nurse who comes every morning, and family members — often abroad — trying to understand what's happening from a phone call.

The product is a shared health record that every person in a patient's care circle can access from their phone. No special hardware. No app store. It works on any Android or iPhone.

When a nurse logs an observation in plain English — "BP 160/95, patient seemed drowsy" — Tessera's AI agent reads it, flags the reading as clinically significant, creates a task for the doctor to review before the next visit, and notifies the family. Automatically.

When the doctor opens the app before a house call, they see a pre-generated brief with exact vital readings, a list of every nurse observation since the last visit, any medication changes, and suggested questions to ask. Generated in four seconds.

When a family member uploads a paper lab report from Dr. Lal PathLabs — a photograph from a phone — Tessera's vision AI parses every test result in 15 seconds. Every value flagged against reference ranges. Everything stored and searchable. No typing.

If there's an emergency, one QR code scan gives any paramedic or ER doctor instant access to the patient's full medical record — name, blood type, allergies, current medications, emergency contacts, critical alerts — with no login required.

This is the system that Indian home care has never had. The doctors were good. The nurses were trained. The families cared. The coordination was broken. Tessera fixes that.

# HOW IT WORKS SECTION

## Section headline
> **Set up in 5 minutes. Running in the background forever.**

## Steps

**Step 1 — Create your record**
Enter the basics: name, date of birth, blood type, conditions, allergies, current medications. Takes about 5 minutes. You can add more later — the record grows as care happens.

**Step 2 — Share with your care team**
Give your nurse and your doctor a code. They link their account in 30 seconds. Your family abroad? Same code, instant access. Everyone sees the same record, from their own phone.

**Step 3 — Log. The AI does the rest.**
Anyone in the care circle can log an observation in plain English. The AI reads every entry, extracts clinically relevant information, and routes it to the right person. The nurse logs. The doctor gets briefed. The family gets notified. No one has to chase anyone.

**Step 4 — Everything in one place**
Lab reports, prescriptions, wound photos, appointment history, handover notes — uploaded once, accessible to everyone, parsed by AI so nothing gets buried.

# FEATURES SECTION

## Section headline
> **Everything your care team needs. Nothing it doesn't.**

---

### Feature 1 — AI Care Coordination
**The agent that never goes off-shift**

When a nurse logs "BP 160/90, patient seemed confused," Tessera's AI agent:
- Flags the reading as watch-level
- Creates a task for the doctor to review
- Sends the care team an alert
- Updates the patient's health status

No one had to route it. No one had to call. It just happened.

The agent is role-aware. It knows whether the message came from a doctor, a nurse, or a family member. It responds and acts accordingly — clinical detail for the doctor, practical next steps for the nurse, reassurance for the family.

---

### Feature 2 — Doctor's Pre-Visit Brief
**Walk into every visit knowing exactly what happened**

Before every house call or appointment, the doctor gets a generated brief:
- Exact vital readings with dates and citations
- Every nurse observation since the last visit
- Medication changes in the last 30 days
- Drug interaction alerts
- Suggested discussion points
- Follow-up items extracted from previous notes

Generated in 4 seconds from the patient's live record. Not from what the patient remembered to mention.

---

### Feature 3 — Carer Shift Briefing
**The handover that actually happens**

When a nurse or carer starts a shift, Tessera generates a personalised briefing:
- What's changed since the last visit
- Prioritised checklist of what to check today
- Any flagged issues requiring attention
- Emergency notes if anything critical is active

At the end of the shift, they complete a structured handover — checklist, vitals, notes — and the AI generates a clean summary for the next carer. No more notebook on the bedside table. No more dropped handovers.

---

### Feature 4 — Lab Report Parsing
**Your paper lab report, digitised in 15 seconds**

Photograph any lab report — Dr. Lal PathLabs, Thyrocare, SRL, hospital printout. Tessera's vision AI reads it.

Every test result extracted. Abnormal values flagged in red. Normal ranges shown for context. Everything stored in the patient's record, searchable, and visible to the full care team.

FBC. LFT. U&E. Lipids. TFTs. HbA1c. And more.

No transcription. No typing. 15 seconds.

---

### Feature 5 — Emergency QR Code
**Critical information for the moment it matters most**

Every patient gets a QR code. If there's an emergency, any paramedic or ER doctor scans it — no login, no app, no account.

They see instantly:
- Full name, date of birth, blood type
- Current medications with doses
- All known allergies and reactions
- Active conditions
- Emergency contacts with phone numbers
- Critical alerts (DNR, pacemaker, implants, etc.)

The card refreshes automatically as the patient's record updates. Always current. Always accessible.

---

### Feature 6 — Drug Interaction Checking
**Know before you prescribe or dispense**

Tessera runs a clinical drug interaction analysis across the full medication list whenever a new medication is added. Interactions are classified as:

- **Contraindicated** — must not be combined
- **Major** — serious risk, doctor review required
- **Moderate** — monitor carefully

Both patient-friendly and clinical descriptions provided. Recommendations for each interaction. Immediate allergy-drug conflict detection — if a prescribed medication matches a recorded allergy, it's flagged before it's added.

---

### Feature 7 — Prescription Scanning
**From handwritten Rx to digital record in seconds**

Photograph any prescription — printed or handwritten, English or Hindi, typed or scrawled. Tessera's vision AI extracts every medication with dose, frequency, route, and prescriber name.

Medications are reviewed before being added — the system checks interactions with existing meds first. One tap to add to the record.

Family doctors in India often write prescriptions quickly and by hand. Tessera reads them anyway.

---

### Feature 8 — Care Team Messaging
**One thread. Every person in the circle.**

A real-time messaging thread connects the patient, their nurse, their doctor, and their family. Everyone can see the full conversation.

Type **@ai** anywhere to ask Tessera's AI about the patient's records:
- "What was her last BP reading?"
- "Any drug interactions with the new medication?"
- "What did the nurse report this week?"
- "Can we resolve the wound issue?"

The AI responds with exact data from the record, not generic answers. And it can take action directly from the thread — create tasks, flag issues, resolve concerns.

---

### Feature 9 — Ask My Records
**Your entire health history, answerable in plain English**

Ask anything about the patient's record in natural language. Tessera searches across logs, documents, lab reports, medications, and vitals to answer.

- "What medications was she on three months ago?"
- "Any abnormal results in the last blood test?"
- "When was the last time she had a physiotherapy appointment?"
- "What did the last three nurses report about her appetite?"

Answers come from the actual data, with source citations.

---

### Feature 10 — Wound & Issue Tracking
**For conditions that need watching visit by visit**

Active health issues — wounds, post-surgical recovery, chronic conditions being monitored — are tracked as structured issues, not buried in log text.

Carers can attach photos. The AI analyses the progression across observations:
- Is it improving or worsening?
- What's the current stage estimate?
- What should the carer watch for?
- Does it need escalation?

When the doctor or nurse marks an issue resolved, the AI confirms with its own assessment and the record closes cleanly.

---

### Feature 11 — Vitals Tracking
**Every reading, in context**

Vitals are extracted from log text automatically — BP, heart rate, SpO2, temperature, blood glucose, weight, pain score. No separate form to fill. The nurse writes "BP 148/92, HR 76, patient complained of mild headache" and all three values are extracted and stored.

Trends visualised over 30 days. Threshold alerts: if a reading is clinically significant, it's flagged without anyone having to check a reference chart.

---

### Feature 12 — Document Intelligence
**Upload once. AI reads everything.**

Upload any medical document — discharge summaries, imaging reports, GP letters, specialist notes, test results. Tessera extracts:

- Document type and date
- Issuing doctor and facility
- Key clinical findings
- Any diagnoses mentioned
- Follow-up instructions
- Urgent items requiring action

Everything searchable. Everything visible to the care team.

# TRUST & SECURITY SECTION

## Section headline
> **Your health data. Your control.**

### Data points
- **Patient-owned records** — you control all sharing. Revoke access anytime.
- **Encrypted in transit and at rest** — Firebase with AES-256 encryption
- **No AI training on your data** — Azure OpenAI processes your queries but does not train on patient information
- **India & EU data residency** — data stays in your region
- **Audit trail** — every access and change is logged with timestamp and user
- **Secure sharing** — patient codes use Firestore security rules; no one accesses data they're not explicitly linked to
- **Emergency access** — time-limited QR tokens expire after 7 days
